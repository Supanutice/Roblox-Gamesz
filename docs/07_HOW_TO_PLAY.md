# How to Play — Anime Warriors III

## First Launch
1. Open the project in Roblox Studio (`rojo serve` + connect via the Rojo plugin) and press **Play**.
2. You spawn in **Hamato Village** on Beginner Island with your first hero,
   **Kaito Embren** (Fire Fighter), already equipped.
3. The **green ring** around every village is a safe zone — no enemy or boss
   can target you inside it.

## Controls (PC)

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left Click | M1 attack — 4-hit combo, finisher knocks back |
| Right Click | Heavy attack — 0.55s charge, breaks normal block; late in a combo it launches for air combos |
| Q | Dash (0.2s of invulnerability frames) |
| 1 / 2 | Hero Skill 1 / Skill 2 |
| R | **Ultimate** (requires full yellow energy bar, 100) |
| F (hold) | Block (-65% damage). Blocking within 0.18s of a hit = **Perfect Block** (100% + counter window) |
| Tab | Target lock |
| C / B / K / L / V / P | Characters / Inventory / Summon / Quests / Party / PvP |

Mobile: Attack / Heavy / Dash / S1 / S2 / ULT / Block buttons appear
automatically (block is hold-to-block).

## Key Systems
- **Energy & Awakening** — gain energy from M1 hits (+4), skill hits (+8) and
  taking damage. Cast 2 ultimates in one life to trigger **Awakening**
  (+35% damage for 20s).
- **Elements** — Fire > Ice > Wind > Earth > Lightning > Water > Fire, and
  Light ↔ Dark. Advantage = ×1.3 damage, disadvantage = ×0.75.
- **Status effects** — Burn/Poison/Bleed (damage over time), Freeze/Stun
  (disabled), Shock (slow + defense down). Particle color shows the type.

## Core Loop
1. **Talk to village NPCs** (walk up, hold Talk) — quests are accepted and
   turned in automatically; track them in the Quest Log (L).
2. **Hunt monsters** outside the green ring for XP, gold and materials.
3. **Summon (K)** — 100 gems per pull / 950 per 10-pull. Soft pity from
   pull 50; **guaranteed Legendary+ by pull 80**; Mythic pity at 400.
4. **Grow your heroes (C)** — Level Up (gold), Evolve (materials from bosses),
   Ascend (shards from duplicates). Equip Slot 1 to play as them — your
   character visually morphs into the hero.
5. **Dungeons** — enter the **purple portal** near each village (party up to
   4 via V). Survive the waves; the final wave is a red ELITE. Rewards:
   Raid Tokens + evolution materials.
6. **World bosses** — cross the bridges to the pillar arena at the far end of
   each island. Multi-phase fights; **dodge the red telegraph circles**.
   Defeating the boss unlocks the next world.
7. **Step on every blue waypoint pad** to unlock Fast Travel.
8. **PvP (P)** — queue Ranked 1v1 / Casual / 3v3. Auto-teleport to the arena,
   best of 3, climb Bronze → Grandmaster.

## World Progression
Beginner Island (1–25) → Shinobi Nation (25–70) → Soul Empire (70–140) →
Grand Ocean (140–230) → Demon Realm (230–350) → Celestial Kingdom (350–500)

## Tips
- Hunt the hidden **golden chests (✦)** scattered around every island.
- Daily/Weekly quests auto-accept on login — remember to CLAIM in L.
- Falling into the ocean is safe: the current returns you to shore.
- Bosses idle until someone enters their arena — buff up first.
- Support heroes (e.g. Mira Tidesong) make dungeon runs far easier.
